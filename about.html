About Ubuntu Community Support

Working together to support individuals, families and communities in need.

Who We Are

Ubuntu Community Support is a non-profit organisation focused on providing assistance to people who require support within the community. Our organisation aims to identify beneficiaries' needs and provide appropriate assistance.

Our Mission and Vision

🎯

Our Mission

To provide meaningful assistance to individuals and families in need while promoting dignity, care and community involvement.

🌍

Our Vision

To build stronger communities where people have access to the support and resources they need to improve their lives.

🤝

Our Values

We promote compassion, respect, teamwork, responsibility and equality when assisting members of our community.

What We Do

Ubuntu Community Support assists beneficiaries according to their individual needs. Assistance may include food support, hygiene products, educational resources, clothing and emergency support.


Beneficiary information is collected through the registration process so that the organisation can understand the type of assistance required.

We Believe in Ubuntu

By supporting one another, we can make a positive difference in our communities.
